Rules and registered IDs
-----------------------
0 floor; 1 authored start (the dot is a separate object); 2 matching-entry goal;
3 wall; 70 White crate. Targets 23,24,25 control links 1,2,3; phase walls
10,26,27 are their corresponding gates. IDs 4,8,30 are NOT the crate/target/gate
legend for this pack. Use the live registry and Tile Mechanics Reference.

This pack's crates are explicitly pushable and not pullable. A complete push
commits crate and dot together, then derives target occupancy. Targets activate
only under same-cell crates, never by dot occupancy or adjacent crates. Targets
are non-blocking features, not walls. Each target's momentary link remains open
while its crate stays parked. Requested closure under a crate is pending until
departure; closure beneath the dot fails the attempt. Intended routes avoid
that situation. All P/W carriers remain White and all starts are safe.

Minimum-stroke progression (all proved and replayed)
--------------------------------------------------
1. First Push          5×5   PAR 8   One target, one gate, safe raised detour.
2. Corridor Pivot      6×6   PAR 8   Target push plus a safe blocker pocket.
3. Corner Check        8×6   PAR 9   Read sealed corners; no early deadlock trap.
4. Two-Chamber Detour 10×6   PAR 13  Two targets/links in sequence.
5. Warehouse No. 3    12×8   PAR 15  Warehouse homage, two staged target crates.
6. Long Alley Remix  14×6   PAR 17  Remote target gate, sparse two-way detour.
7. Four-Crate Shuffle 16×9  PAR 21  Three targets plus one safe parked blocker.
8. Master Warehouse  16×9   PAR 23  Three links, blocker, final switchback.

Every target and gate contributes to its minimum route; removing any individual
target makes that board unsolvable with its gate retained. These are not scores
inferred from an old version or personal bests. The complete world minimum is
114 strokes. Mathematical proofs do not replace lesson or browser review.

Safety and deliberate deviations from old concept sketches
---------------------------------------------------------
Levels 1–4 have complete bounded reachable-state review with no unwinnable
playing states. Their target overshoot walls and blocker pocket guard wrong
pushes. Level 3 intentionally teaches reading corners, not suffering an
unannounced irreversible tutorial reset.

Levels 5 and 8 retain avoidable irreversible wrong pushes as advanced planning
consequences; these are nonterminal mistakes recoverable with undo/restart.
Levels 6 and 7 have no unwinnable reachable playing states. The irreversibly
parked blocker in level 7 is useful, not a level deadlock. Counts and example
mistakes are recorded in the full route review.

No timed gate, conveyor pull, crushing, breakable wall, or alternate target
mechanic is implemented here. The long alley remixes distance rather than
introducing the old blueprint's belt/pull behavior. "Warehouse No. 3" is a
homage, not a historical-layout reproduction claim. Larger levels preserve
their envelope without pretending blank wall rows add puzzle complexity.
